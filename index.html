What Is Data?

Data is a collection of facts, numbers, or observations that can be analyzed to find patterns, answer questions, or support decisions. In programming, we store data in variables, lists, and dictionaries. The key to becoming a strong programmer and thinker is learning how to collect, organize, and interpret data meaningfully.
Types of Data
• Quantitative (numerical) — things you can count or measure: temperature, scores, speed, population.
• Qualitative (categorical) — things you can describe or label: color, genre, city name, opinion.
